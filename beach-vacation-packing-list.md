## Packing Tips to Save Money

1. **Borrow or already own:** Swimsuits, shoes, regular clothes
2. **Buy in bulk travel sizes:** Toiletries are cheaper than full-size hotel bottles
3. **Skip expensive resort gear:** Rent snorkel equipment at the beach instead of buying
4. **DIY sun protection:** Lightweight long sleeves are often cheaper than high-end rashguards
5. **Use hotel amenities:** Most resorts provide basic toiletries; pack less
6. **Multi-purpose items:** A sarong works as beach cover, towel, and evening wrap

## Pro Packing Checklist

- [ ] Do a trial packing 2–3 days before
- [ ] Check weather forecast for your destination
- [ ] Confirm luggage size limits with airline
- [ ] Leave room for souvenirs/purchases
- [ ] Pack heavy items at the bottom, delicates on top
- [ ] Keep essentials (sunscreen, phone charger) in carry-on
